Why this exists

Growing up I spent a lot of time looking at plugins I could not have. The ones with the good panels, the ones everyone used. I did not have the money, and for a long while I did not have the skill either.

At some point I stopped waiting and learned to write them. Vexx Audio is the result: my own processors, given away with the source open, because I remember what it was like to want them and not have them.

No account. No licence key. No trial timer.
